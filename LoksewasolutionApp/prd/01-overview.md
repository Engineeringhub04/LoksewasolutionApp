# 1. Project Overview

## 1.1 Introduction
Loksewa Solution is a mobile learning platform built for Nepal's Loksewa (public service) examination aspirants. It centralizes study material, practice questions, mock exams, current affairs, and a community discussion space into a single premium, polished mobile experience.

## 1.2 Problem Statement
Loksewa aspirants rely on fragmented resources — scattered PDF notes over Facebook/Telegram groups, separate apps for current affairs, printed question banks, and no centralized progress tracking. No single trustworthy app brings structured notes, practice, mock exams, current affairs, and peer discussion together.

## 1.3 Vision
Become Nepal's leading, most trusted digital preparation platform for Loksewa and other competitive government exams — the app an aspirant opens every day from the start of preparation to exam day.

## 1.4 Mission
Deliver a premium-quality, easy-to-use, reliable study companion helping every aspirant study smarter, practice consistently, and track measurable progress toward passing.
